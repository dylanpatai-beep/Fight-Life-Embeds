# MA-01 — THE SOURCE MAP

**Subject** 1 · Martial arts (`public`) · **Module** 1 of 8
**Apply period** 1 set (~16 days) · **Opened** 2026-08-09 · **Status** OPEN

---

## SCOPE

Assemble the real source map for martial arts — the people, books, videos, and rooms you
actually learn from — and publish the map itself.

## THE QUESTION THIS MODULE ANSWERS

> Who am I actually learning this from, and what does the map of this subject look like?

## WHY THIS IS MODULE 1 AND NOT A STALL

You can't cite sources you've never listed. Every module after this one names sources; none
of them can be filled until this exists. The alternative was me inventing a reading list,
which isn't happening.

It also ships something in your first set. You publish before you've finished a single
technique breakdown.

---

## SOURCES

Self-generated. That's the point — this module's work *is* producing the source list.

Pull from: your coaches and the corrections they actually give you · your shelf · your watch
history · the fighters whose tape you rewatch · the training partner who explains things
well · anything you've saved and never opened.

## THE GATE

A module is DONE when all three are true. Shipping the output CLOSES it and opens MA-02.

- [ ] **G1 — Worksheet + notes complete.** All six pages of
      `curriculum/worksheets/MA-01-source-map.pdf`, filled by hand.
- [ ] **G2 — Taught to someone.** Name + date logged on worksheet p.4.
- [ ] **G3 — Applied for one set.** Start date + end date on p.5.
- [ ] **CLOSE — Output shipped.** URL on p.6.

### What "applied" means for this module

Use the map to decide what you study, every day of the set. Then notice what you reached for
that *wasn't* on it — that gap is the most valuable thing this module produces, and it goes
on p.5.

### Teach-back — the specific move

Show the map to your coach or a training partner and ask what's missing.

That conversation *is* the teach-back, and it will improve the map. Log what they said was
missing, not just that you did it. This is a conversation, not a filmed thing — the gym's
filming limits don't apply here.

**Ask one more thing in the same conversation: what is the gym's weekly technique
progression?** You think there is one but it isn't confirmed. That answer decides the entire
MA-02→MA-08 sequence — their order replaces my draft. It costs you one extra question inside
a conversation you're already having. It's prompted on worksheet p.4.

## THE DAILY REP

Same every day. Open the worksheet, do the next unchecked line, write STOPPED AT, close it.

| Day type | What one rep tends to get you |
|---|---|
| **Floor** (post-48/72, wrecked) | **Name one source. One line. Done.** Still counts. |
| **Shift day** (15–30 min) | Two or three sources with a note each, or one page's worth of thinking |
| **Rotation off day** (~45 min) | One worksheet page complete |
| **Deep day** (2–4 hrs) | Distillation, teach-back, draft the newsletter, ship, open MA-02 |

**You never choose a mode.** You do the rep. How far you get tells you what kind of day it
was.
