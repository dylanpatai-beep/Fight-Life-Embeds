DEPTH LADDER

// DECIDE THE CEILING BEFORE YOU START

You are not going to master ten things. Setting a ceiling per topic is the highest-leverage decision in the system. Aiming L1 at something is not failure — it's the decision that makes L4 possible somewhere else.

L4 · AUTHORITY Produces original work. Other people cite you. YEARS
L3 · PRACTITIONER Can coach someone else through it. Handles the edge cases. 300+ HRS
L2 · APPLIED Can do the thing unsupervised under normal conditions. 50–100 HRS
L1 · LITERATE Knows the map and the vocabulary. Can hold a real conversation. ~10 HRS

NOTION
THE NON-NEGOTIABLE You write the compression. Claude grades it. The moment you let AI write the one-pager, you've automated away the only step that was making you smarter — and it will feel more productive than ever. Prompt it like this: "Here's my one-pager on X. Don't rewrite it. Find what I got wrong, what I hand-waved, and what I left out that a practitioner would consider essential."

BETTER QUESTIONS

// "SUMMARIZE THIS" IS THE WEAKEST PROMPT YOU OWN
Instead ofAskWhy it works
"Summarize this"What does this source assume that it never states?Surfaces the frame, which is where the real content lives
"Explain X"Where do these two sources actually disagree, and who's right?Conflict is the fastest route to understanding a field
"Key takeaways"Give me the 5 questions I must answer to prove I understand this.Converts reading into a test you can fail

THREE LANES

// TEN-TOPIC CURRICULA DIE BECAUSE ALL TEN OPEN AT ONCE
LANE A
DEEP
1 TOPIC · 5 HRS/WEEK · 12 WEEKS

One 12-week cycle producing a flagship artifact. Rotates every quarter. Only one topic is ever in Lane A. This is where deliberate study time is spent.

LANE B
WORKING
2 TOPICS · 0 EXTRA HRS

Already inside your daily life — training, the job, food. No study time allocated. The work is documentation, not study. Cheapest content you'll ever make.

LANE C
AMBIENT
EVERYTHING ELSE · NO OBLIGATION

Podcasts on the drive, rabbit holes, books before bed. Capture only. Nothing in Lane C is allowed to generate guilt. It feeds the inbox so Lane A never starts cold.

THE ARITHMETIC Four quarters a year × 3 years = 12 deep cycles. Every topic gets one; the important ones get two. That's a real curriculum with a real end date — instead of ten topics permanently at 15%.

ON BUILDING THE APP

// THE ANSWER IS NOT YET
THE HONEST VERSION Building a learning app is the most satisfying way to avoid learning. It feels like the highest-leverage move and is reliably the lowest — you'd spend the first cycle building a container instead of putting anything in one. And you'd build the wrong thing, because you don't yet know what the system needs. You'd be designing from imagination instead of from friction.

The rule: the app gets built after two completed 12-week cycles, and only for the specific friction those cycles surfaced.

The reframe: the app is a content topic, not a prerequisite. Building it in public is a Lane A cycle under #09 and #10 — every session is a receipt. You get the app and the content, but only if you sequence it that way instead of building it first and silently.
